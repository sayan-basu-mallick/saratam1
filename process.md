# Saratam Digiplex — Client Process

## Overview

Saratam Digiplex follows a requirement-based approach to projects.

Every project is different, so the exact process, timeline, technology, deliverables and cost may vary depending on the client's requirements.

Our general workflow is:

Inquiry → Requirement Discussion → Requirement Analysis → Estimate → Confirmation → Planning → Development/Execution → Testing & Review → Delivery → Support

Clients can contact Saratam Digiplex through WhatsApp, phone, email or other available communication channels.

# 5. Estimate & Quotation

After understanding the requirements, Saratam Digiplex can provide an estimated project cost.

A basic project may start from a lower price, while projects involving advanced features, custom development, integrations, AI, backend systems or complex UI/UX may cost significantly more.

The final price depends on the actual scope of work.

For this reason, prices shown by the AI assistant should be treated as approximate starting estimates unless a specific quotation has been provided.

The chatbot should encourage serious prospects to contact Saratam Digiplex directly for an accurate quotation.
