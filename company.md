# Saratam Digiplex — Company Information

## About Saratam Digiplex

**Saratam Digiplex** is a digital marketing and technology company based in **Bally, Howrah, West Bengal, India**.

Saratam Digiplex provides digital marketing, technology, creative and online-growth solutions to businesses, startups, entrepreneurs, organizations and individuals who want to establish, improve and grow their digital presence.

The company combines **digital marketing, technology, creativity, design and audience-focused strategy** to provide businesses with solutions under one roof.

Its services cover areas including digital marketing, search engine optimization, social media marketing, paid advertising, website development, web applications, mobile application development, Flutter development, UI/UX design, branding, graphic design, content, lead generation, business automation and other digital solutions.

Saratam Digiplex aims to act as a **full-service digital partner**, rather than simply providing one isolated digital service.

---

## Company Name

The company is called **Saratam Digiplex**.

The name **Saratam** has a personal meaning. It combines parts of the names of the founder's family members:

* **Sa** from Sayan
* **Rat** from Ratna
* **Am** from Ambuj

The name therefore represents a personal connection between the founder, his mother and his father.

## Founded

Saratam Digiplex was founded in **2023** by **Sayan Basu Mallick**, who serves as the company's **Founder & Managing Director**.

The company was created after years of the founder's experience in social media, content creation, audience development, online communities, digital promotion and traffic generation.

The journey behind Saratam Digiplex began with creating and distributing digital content, building large online audiences and understanding how people interact with content on digital platforms.

This experience eventually evolved into a broader vision of helping businesses use technology and digital platforms for growth.
