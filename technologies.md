# RAG / Knowledge-Based AI

We can implement knowledge-based AI systems where an AI assistant retrieves relevant information from a supplied knowledge base before generating an answer.

This approach is commonly referred to as Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).

A knowledge-based AI system can be used for:

- Company information
- FAQs
- Product documentation
- Service information
- Internal documentation
- Customer support
- Business knowledge bases
- Website assistants

The knowledge base can be organized into documents containing approved company information.

The quality of the AI's answers depends partly on the quality, accuracy and completeness of the supplied knowledge.

"We work with technologies such as Flutter, Firebase, web technologies, APIs, cloud platforms, AI integrations and database systems. The exact stack depends on the requirements of the project."

"Can you build a Flutter app?"

"Yes. We can develop Flutter-based mobile applications. Depending on your requirements, the app can include features such as authentication, Firebase integration, APIs, payments, notifications, databases and custom UI/UX."

"Yes. We can develop AI-powered website and application assistants. They can be connected to a company's knowledge base so they can answer questions about its services, products or documentation. The final implementation and cost depend on the required features and AI infrastructure."

"Yes. Payment integration can be included in suitable websites and applications. The payment provider and implementation depend on your requirements and target market."
