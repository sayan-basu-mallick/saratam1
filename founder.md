# About the Founder

## Sayan Basu Mallick

**Founder & Managing Director, Saratam Digiplex**

Sayan Basu Mallick is the Founder and Managing Director of Saratam Digiplex. His professional journey began long before the creation of the digital agency, through social media, content creation, online communities and digital audience development.

## Early Social Media Journey

Around 2015, Sayan began creating and managing several popular Bengali creative Facebook pages, including:

- Hasir Foyara
- Shesh Chithi
- The Song of Life

He was among the early creators, writers and page administrators involved in the emerging Facebook page culture in India.

One of the major milestones of his early social-media career came through **Shesh Chithi**. Shortly after the page was launched in 2016, one of his original Bengali creative posts went viral and received nearly **1 lakh shares**.

According to Sayan's experience and records from the Bengali creative-page community, he considers this one of the defining achievements of his early social-media career. The experience demonstrated his ability to create content that could resonate with a very large audience organically.

His other pages, particularly **Hasir Foyara**, also developed substantial audiences. Over the years, his pages collectively grew to an audience of approximately **30 lakh (3 million) followers**.

## Writing and Content Creation

Beyond managing Facebook pages, Sayan established himself as a writer and content creator on Facebook.

His personal Facebook profile grew to nearly **50,000 followers**. His writings, thoughts and creative posts have received strong organic engagement, with many posts becoming viral and several achieving exceptionally high numbers of likes and shares.

His writings have also reached audiences internationally. According to his experience, some popular content creators have directly used his posts and writings as scripts for their own videos, further extending the reach of his work.

Through these experiences, Sayan developed extensive first-hand knowledge of:

- Audience psychology
- Viral content
- Organic reach
- Social-media growth
- Content creation
- Audience engagement
- Community building
- Social-media distribution

## From Creator to Digital Traffic Strategist

In **2016**, Sayan worked with **WittyFeed**, an international web portal.

He used the audiences he had built across his Facebook pages to distribute links to WittyFeed articles and news stories.

By strategically sharing and promoting those links through his pages and communities, he generated millions of visits and significant traffic to the website during that period.

This experience gave him first-hand understanding of how:

- Content
- Audience behaviour
- Social distribution
- Traffic generation
- Audience engagement

could work together at scale.

He also used his pages and communities to promote movies, brands and various digital campaigns, further developing his understanding of social-media promotion, audience engagement and organic reach.

This stage of his career became an important foundation for what would eventually become Saratam Digiplex.

His journey evolved from creating content and building audiences to understanding how digital platforms could be used to generate measurable reach and traffic for businesses and publishers.

## Founding Saratam Digiplex

In **2023**, after years of working with content, audiences, social media and digital promotion, Sayan decided to take the next step and founded **Saratam Digiplex**.

The name **Saratam** has a personal meaning. It combines parts of the names of his family members:

- **Sa** from Sayan
- **Rat** from his mother Ratna
- **Am** from his father Ambuj

This makes Saratam a personal part of the company's identity.

## Vision

Sayan's vision for Saratam Digiplex is:

> High-quality digital services should not be accessible only to companies with large budgets.

With this philosophy, he began building a team and network of professionals across different areas of the digital industry.

The network includes professionals working in areas such as:

- Digital Marketing
- Social Media Strategy
- Graphic and Visual Design
- Web Development
- Mobile App Development
- Search Engine Optimization (SEO)
- Performance Marketing
- Content Writing and Content Creation
- UI/UX Design
- Branding
- Analytics
- Conversion Optimization

The goal is to create a **full-service digital partner** capable of helping businesses with technology, creativity, marketing and online growth while keeping services accessible to startups, small businesses and growing brands.

## Professional Philosophy

For Sayan, Saratam Digiplex represents the natural progression of a journey that began with creating content and building communities, evolved into driving large volumes of online traffic and promoting brands, and eventually grew into building complete digital solutions for businesses.

His approach is based on:

- Understanding people
- Creating meaningful content
- Using technology intelligently
- Understanding digital audiences
- Building online visibility
- Turning attention into opportunity
